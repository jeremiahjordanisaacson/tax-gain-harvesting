Tax-Gain Harvesting

A plain-English guide and an interactive training quest for harvesting long-term capital gains inside the federal 0% tax bracket.

Tax-gain harvesting is the deliberate act of selling appreciated investments to realize long-term gains during a low-income year — filling up the federal 0% long-term capital-gains bracket — then (optionally) buying back in to reset your cost basis higher. Unlike tax-loss harvesting, the wash-sale rule generally isn't in your way, because you're realizing a gain.

It's one of the most valuable moves in the "gap years" between retiring and the arrival of pensions, Social Security, and required retirement-account distributions. Read the guide first, then walk the quest to make it stick.
